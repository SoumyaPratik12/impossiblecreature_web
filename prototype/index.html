How to play

  • Left-click a blue unit to select it; left-drag empty ground to box-select units. Hold Shift to add to the selection.
  • Drag a selected unit to reposition it; choose Compact, Line or Column, then right-click to move the selected group. Units route around marked rocks.
  • Units automatically engage nearby opponents, including while moving (attack-move).
  • Space + drag or middle-mouse drag to pan the camera.
  • Scroll to zoom around your pointer, or use the zoom controls.
  • Human workers are shown as small figures. Assign them to collect timber and ore and deliver loads to your headquarters or storage.
    • \n
  • Build a land, water or air laboratory. Choose a production domain in Creature Builder before queueing a matching creature.
    • \n
  • Both teams have workers and can build domain-specific creature facilities. The enemy uses a basic scripted production strategy.
  • Destroy the enemy base to win; protect your own base. Difficulty changes enemy economy and defensive fire.
  • Use Save state to store a local prototype snapshot. RTS workers, resources, buildings and production queues are not yet included in save files.
