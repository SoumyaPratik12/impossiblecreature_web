How to play

  • Left-click a blue unit to select it; drag it to reposition directly.
  • With a unit selected, click empty ground or right-click to issue a move order.
  • Units automatically engage nearby opponents.
  • Shift + drag or middle-mouse drag to pan the camera.
  • Scroll to zoom around your pointer, or use the zoom controls.
  • Use Save state to store a local prototype snapshot.
